COUNTER ATTACK

Play your own copy of Counter-Strike 1.6
Starting…
Counter Attack needs your own Counter-Strike 1.6 files. They stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded or shared with anyone.
  1. On a computer, open Steam, right-click Counter-Strike → Manage → Browse local files.
  2. Select the cstrike and valve folders and zip them together (right-click → Compress / Send to → Compressed folder).
  3. Choose that .zip here. You only do this once per device. (On iPad, save the zip to Files first.)
Runs on the open-source Xash3D FWGS engine. Needs a keyboard and mouse (an iPad keyboard with trackpad works).
