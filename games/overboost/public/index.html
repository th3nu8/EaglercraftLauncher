      Moves worth knowing

      • Hold jump for a higher jump. Press it again in the air for a second jump.
      • Hold a direction and press jump in the air to flip that way. A forward flip into the ball is the hardest shot you have.
      • Hold slide while turning to swing the back end round without losing speed.
      • Small pads give 12 boost, the six big ones fill the tank. Above 80 km/h you can demolish opponents.
      • You can drive up the walls. Jump off them to reach high balls.
